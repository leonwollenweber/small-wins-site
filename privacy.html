This policy explains how the Android app Small Wins (com.lewo.smallwins) handles information.

2. Information stored by the app

Small Wins stores the information you enter in the app, including goal names, descriptions, measures, wins, notes, and user-defined conversions. It also stores appearance choices and language settings.

This information is kept in the app's private storage on your Android device. The controller does not operate an account system or a server that receives your goals, wins, or notes.

3. Android backup and device transfer

Small Wins allows Android to include its private app data in Android-managed cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Whether this happens depends on your device, Android version, backup provider, and account settings.

Backup and transfer services are provided by Android, Google, or your device manufacturer's backup provider under that provider's terms and privacy policy. The controller cannot access or control a backup stored in your personal backup account.

You can manage backup settings through your Android device or Google account.

4. Optional external support through PayPal

Small Wins includes an optional “Support the app” screen. It does not load payment products, connect to a payment service in the background, or process payments inside the app. If you select “Open PayPal,” Android opens this fixed PayPal link outside Small Wins:

Open the PayPal support page

No goal, win, note, device identifier, account identifier, or other user-entered app data is added to this link. Small Wins receives no payment status, payment-card information, bank details, or other financial information from PayPal and cannot determine whether you complete a payment.

If you use PayPal, PayPal processes the information you provide there under its own terms and privacy statement. As the payment recipient, the controller may receive transaction information that PayPal makes available, such as your name, email address, amount, note, and transaction ID. The controller uses that information only for payment support, accounting, fraud prevention, and compliance with legal obligations.

PayPal Privacy Statement
PayPal User Agreement

5. Advertising and analytics

Small Wins does not contain advertising or a behavioral analytics service. The controller does not sell your personal data or share your goal content with advertisers. Google Play and your Android backup provider may process technical, account, device, or diagnostic information as part of providing their respective platform services. PayPal processes information only after you choose to leave the app and use PayPal's external service.

6. Retention and deletion

Goal data and settings remain in the app's private storage until you delete them, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app. You can delete individual wins and goals inside Small Wins.

Android may retain a backup after the local app copy is deleted and may restore it after a later installation. You can manage or delete device backups through your Android or Google account settings. PayPal and the controller retain transaction information according to their respective policies and applicable financial or legal retention requirements.

7. Security

Small Wins relies on Android's private app storage to protect locally stored information. Network communication used by Android-managed backup and, after you leave the app, PayPal is handled by the respective platform provider. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your choices and questions

You can use the core goal-tracking features without making a contribution. You can delete local content in the app and manage Android backup through your device or account settings.

For privacy questions or requests concerning information handled directly by the controller, contact leonwollenweber.dev@gmail.com.

9. Hosting of this privacy-policy page

This privacy-policy page is hosted using GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub. When you open the page, GitHub may process technical and usage information such as your IP address, device and browser information, referring page, requested pages, and the date and time of requests under GitHub's own privacy statement.

The controller does not add JavaScript, advertising, analytics, forms, tracking pixels, or non-essential third-party content to this website.

GitHub Privacy Statement

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Small Wins changes or when legal or platform requirements change. The current version and its update date will remain available on this page.
